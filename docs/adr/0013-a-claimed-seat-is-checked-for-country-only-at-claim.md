# A Claimed Seat is checked for country only at claim, and never Region-Locks

A Team Licence is sold at the Team Admin's Purchasing-Power Price, and a Student's country is checked against it once, when they claim a Seat. After that, Course Access ignores country for anyone who holds a Claimed Seat on the course, even if they also have an Individual Purchase made at a Purchasing-Power Price elsewhere. An Individual Purchase is checked on every visit because the buyer chose the price for themselves; a Seat was handed to the Student by someone else, and taking the course away from them as they travel would punish them for the Team Admin's purchase.

## Consequences

Region-Locked is decided per Student per course, from their Individual Purchases that have no Approved Refund Request, not from "the Purchase behind the Enrollment" (an Enrollment doesn't record one; see ADR-0004).
