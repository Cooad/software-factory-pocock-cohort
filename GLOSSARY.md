# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to individual learners and to teams. This glossary fixes the words we use for buying courses and giving money back.

## Language

### Purchases

**Team Purchase**:
A purchase of several Seats in one course, bought for other people to use.
_Avoid_: Team licence, bulk purchase

**Seat**:
One person's place in a course, from a Team Purchase. It is **claimed** when someone redeems its link.
_Avoid_: Coupon, licence

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's request to get their money back for a purchase, with a reason in their own words. A person reviews it by hand and approves or declines it.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Chargeback, Reversal

**Refund**:
The money sent back to the learner once a Refund Request is approved. It is always the amount the learner actually paid.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund Window**:
The time in which a learner can raise a Refund Request. It runs until the end of the 14th calendar day after the day of purchase.
_Avoid_: Cancellation window, cooling-off period

**Chargeback**:
A dispute the cardholder raises through their bank or card issuer, outside Cadence's control. It is not the same thing as a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: using it for anything the learner asks Cadence for directly
